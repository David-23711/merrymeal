What we offer
Everything managed,
so you don't have to.
Our platform handles every step — from registration and menu planning to food safety and real-time delivery tracking.
Weekly menu planning
Staff design weekly menus tailored to members' dietary needs, approved by admin before preparation begins.
Real-time delivery tracking
Caregivers receive instant notifications when meals depart the kitchen and can track status in real time.
Food safety compliance
Every kitchen logs ingredient expiry dates and cooking times to ensure full food safety compliance.
Periodic reassessment
Member health and eligibility is regularly reviewed and meal plans adjusted to match changing needs.
Fundraising & donations
Donors contribute one-time or recurring support, keeping MerryMeal's services running for those who need it most.
Accessibility first
One-click large font and high contrast toggles ensure the platform is usable by all members.
How it works
Simple steps to get started
Getting enrolled with MerryMeal takes just a few minutes.
1
Register online
Fill in your personal details, health condition, and dietary needs on our secure portal.
2
Eligibility review
Our staff reviews your application within 2 business days.
3
Meal plan assigned
A weekly meal plan tailored to your dietary needs is prepared by our kitchen partners.
4
Meals delivered
Hot meals arrive Monday to Friday. Your caregiver is notified every step of the way.
